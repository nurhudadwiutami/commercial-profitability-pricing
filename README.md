# Commercial Profitability & Pricing Strategy (The Furniture Story)

## Executive Summary
This repository analyzes a high-volume, low-margin anomaly within the Furniture category. Despite generating substantial sales revenue, the Furniture division yielded a net profit margin of only 2.5%, significantly underperforming relative to other product categories. 

The primary objective of this project is to identify the root causes of profitability erosion and formulate data-backed pricing and discounting strategies.

---

## Key Findings
* **Discount Cannibalization:** Discontinue-level discounts exceeding **30%** significantly eroded net margins without delivering a proportionate increase in sales volume.
* **Category Performance Gap:** The *Technology* and *Office Supplies* categories maintained healthier profit margins with lower price sensitivity to promotional discounts.
* **Margin Recovery Potential:** Multiple Linear Regression modeling indicates that enforcing a strict discount ceiling can restore category profit margins to an estimated **8–12%**.

---

## Tools & Methodology
* **Data Aggregation & Cleaning:** Microsoft Excel
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Microsoft Excel
* **Statistical Modeling:** Multiple Linear Regression, ANOVA, Discount Threshold Mapping
* **Data Visualization:** Looker Studio

---

## Strategic Recommendations
1. **Discount Capping:** Implement a hard discount ceiling of **20%** on all Furniture SKUs.
2. **Cost Structure Review:** Re-evaluate logistics and unit cost allocation for large-dimensional items.
3. **Promotional Budget Re-allocation:** Shift aggressive promotional budgets toward higher-margin categories (*Technology & Office Supplies*).
